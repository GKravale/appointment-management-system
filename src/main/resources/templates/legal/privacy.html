Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What data we collect

We collect the following personal data when you register and use this system:

  • Name and surname
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Appointment history
  • Profile information (bio, location — for providers)
Why we collect it

Your data is used solely to provide the appointment booking service — to match clients with providers, send appointment confirmations and reminders, and allow providers to manage their schedules.

How it is protected

Passwords are stored using BCrypt hashing and are never readable. Access to data is restricted by user role. We do not share your personal data with third parties.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

To exercise these rights, use the account deletion option in your profile settings or contact the system administrator.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Deleted accounts are soft-deleted and fully purged after 30 days.
